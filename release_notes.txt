## New Features

- An ASI feat pick whose item prerequisites are not met by the character's owned or planned items is flagged with a prerequisite-missing error, and the row stays incomplete.
- The ASI feat browser locks feats whose level or item prerequisites the character does not meet and lists required items on the tile. Clicking a locked feat shows a warning instead of selecting it.

## Changes

- During level-up, the compendium browser for item choice advancements disables feats whose prerequisites the character does not meet.
