## Bug Fixes

- Fixed level-up failing with a duplicate _id error when the actor has a hidden invalid embedded item. Invalid items are now skipped when changes are committed to the actor.
- Fixed the Finalize tab not listing chosen trait proficiencies (skills, tools, languages) picked in advancement. Trait advancements in expertise or mastery modes are no longer listed as proficiencies.
- Item choice advancements with a fixed pool that allow drops now show a compendium Browse button for picking items outside the pool. Browsing is locked to the advancement's restrictions, including spell school. Items picked outside the pool show on the tile and survive a re-render. The button works with Enter or Space, and clicking it no longer opens the pool picker.
