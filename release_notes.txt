## Bug Fixes

- A feat picked during level-up applies its fixed ability score increase.
- HP bonuses from active effects show in the HP tab, the level-up preview and the HUD.
- Level-up runs the advancements on features a character already owns that are tied to the class being leveled, at that class's level. Multiclass creation does the same.
- New characters start at full HP after all advancements apply.
