## Breaking Changes

- Requires dnd5e 6.0.0 or newer.

## Bug Fixes

- Item tooltips use the system's tooltip styling again.
- Level-up applies Modify Items advancements automatically.
- Proficiency, resistance and immunity labels in the Compare window and Finalize tab no longer show raw localization keys.
- Species options on the Identity tab show their walking speed tag again.
- Species senses on the Finalize tab show their sense names.
- The Hero Mancer 2 Settings window opens wide enough to fit every tab on one row, and tab labels no longer break mid-label.
- The Purchase Equipment tab respects the Maximum Magic Item Rarity setting again.
