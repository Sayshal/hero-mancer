## New Features

- Advancements that grant or offer spells with more than one allowed spellcasting ability show a required Spellcasting Ability picker in the Advancements tab, and the chosen ability is applied to those spells. Spell choice advancements show the picker only at their first choice level.
- New Hide Subclass Feature List world setting in the Identity group of the Wizard Flow settings tab shows only a subclass's own description in the Identity tab, without the feature list built from its advancements. Subclasses with an empty description still show the full subclass page.

## Changes

- The Purchase Equipment cart quantity is a number input instead of a dropdown, capped at what the remaining gold can afford. Blank or invalid entries revert to the previous value, and a quantity of 0 removes the item from the cart.
