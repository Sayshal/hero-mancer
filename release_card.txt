## Breaking Changes

- Drafts saved before 3.4.0 lose picks made inside granted items.
- Drafts saved before 3.1.1 no longer convert old personality fields.
- Requires dnd5e 6.1.0 or newer.

## New Features

- A class's suggested subclass is preselected in creation and level-up.
- The Biography tab offers starting factions that grant renown and faction membership.
- You can open a player's saved draft read-only from the Players list.

## Bug Fixes

- 9 bugs squashed
